# Digital Twin-Based Supply Chain Analytics

This project presents a Digital Twin framework for Supply Chain Analytics designed to model, simulate, and analyze real-world inventory and logistics operations. The digital twin replicates key supply chain components, including inventory levels, customer demand, replenishment processes, lead times, order backlogs, product returns, and waste generation.

Using simulation-driven analytics, the system enables organizations to evaluate the impact of demand fluctuations, supply disruptions, and operational constraints on overall supply chain performance. The framework supports data-driven decision-making by identifying bottlenecks, optimizing inventory management strategies, and providing actionable insights through visual analytics.

## Key Features

* Digital representation of end-to-end supply chain operations
* Inventory monitoring and demand forecasting analysis
* Replenishment and lead-time simulation
* Order backlog, returns, and waste tracking
* Interactive dashboards and data visualization
* Scenario-based what-if analysis
* Explainable analytics for supply chain optimization

## Technologies Used

* Python
* System Dynamics Modeling
* Data Analytics & Visualization
* Pandas & NumPy
* Dashboard Development
* Machine Learning (Future Scope)

## Research Objective

The primary objective of this project is to develop an explainable Digital Twin framework that enables organizations to understand complex supply chain dynamics, predict operational challenges, and improve inventory planning through simulation-driven insights. By combining digital twin technology with analytical modeling, the framework supports proactive decision-making and enhances supply chain resilience and efficiency.

## Applications

* Supply Chain Optimization
* Inventory Planning and Control
* Logistics Performance Analysis
* Demand and Replenishment Management
* Operational Risk Assessment
* Decision Support Systems
