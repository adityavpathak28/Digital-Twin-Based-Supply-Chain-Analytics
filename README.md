Digital Twin-Based Supply Chain Analytics

This project develops a Digital Twin of a Supply Chain System to simulate and analyze real-world inventory and logistics operations. The digital twin replicates key supply chain components such as inventory levels, customer demand, replenishment processes, lead times, order backlogs, returns, and waste generation.

Using simulation models and data-driven analytics, the system enables users to study the impact of demand fluctuations, supply delays, and operational constraints on overall supply chain performance. The project focuses on identifying bottlenecks, optimizing inventory management, and supporting informed decision-making through visual insights and performance metrics.

Key Features
Digital representation of supply chain operations
Inventory and demand forecasting analysis
Replenishment and lead-time simulation
Backlog and waste tracking
Interactive dashboards and data visualization
Scenario-based decision support
Explainable analytics for supply chain optimization
Technologies
Python
System Dynamics Modeling
Data Analytics & Visualization
Machine Learning (future scope)
Dashboard Development
Research Objective

To build an explainable digital twin framework that helps organizations understand supply chain dynamics, predict operational issues, and improve inventory planning through simulation-driven insights.
